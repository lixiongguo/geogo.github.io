> **论文**：Alexander I. Bobenko, Tim Hoffmann, Boris A. Springborn. [*Minimal surfaces from circle patterns: Geometry from combinatorics*](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2006.164.231). *Annals of Mathematics*, 164(1), 231–264, 2006.

## 概述

本文把**连续极小曲面**置于**等温（isothermic）曲面**框架中，用 **S-等温离散曲面**（正交相交的圆与球）做离散类比，并通过 **Koebe 多面体**与 **Christoffel 对偶**把组合数据（正交圆图案）变成离散极小曲面。核心链条：

$$
\text{连续极小曲面} \Rightarrow \text{Gauss 映射下的曲率线网} \Rightarrow \text{球面正交圆图案} \Rightarrow \text{Koebe 多面体} \Rightarrow \text{离散极小曲面}
$$

## 1. 连续极小曲面

### 1.1 定义与 Plateau 问题

**极小曲面**：平均曲率处处为零（$H=0$）的正则曲面。典型例子包括**正螺面（Helicoid）**与**悬链面（Catenoid）**——二者属于同一**关联族（associated family）**，存在等距形变相连。

![各种离散极小曲面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116173830137.png)



**Plateau 问题**：给定空间中一条封闭曲线 $\gamma$，是否存在以 $\gamma$ 为边界的极小曲面？肥皂膜给出物理实现；存在性/正则性理论（Douglas、Rado 等）是经典分析几何课题。

![Plateau问题与肥皂膜](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116175051041.png)

### 1.2 Lagrange 方程

对图函数 $z=f(x,y)$，极小曲面等价于非线性椭圆方程（Lagrange 方程）：

$$
\boxed{
(1 + f_y^2)\,f_{xx} - 2 f_x f_y\, f_{xy} + (1 + f_x^2)\,f_{yy} = 0
}
$$



> **图注（中文教材）**：…个区域 $D$。Lagrange 利用他所创立的变分法原理证明了：如果在所有定义在区域 $D$ 上、并且在边界 $\partial D$ 上取值相同的函数的图象中 $M$ 的面积最小，则函数 $z=f(x,y)$ 必须满足偏微分方程
>
> $$(1+f_y^2)\,f_{xx} - 2 f_x f_y\, f_{xy} + (1+f_x^2)\,f_{yy} = 0 \tag{1.1}$$
>
> 这就是著名的所谓极小曲面方程。

### 1.3 等温坐标与 Weierstrass–Enneper 表示

极小曲面是**等温曲面**的子类。在**等温参数坐标** $(u,v)$ 下，第一基本形式为

$$
ds^2 = \lambda^2(du^2 + dv^2), \qquad E=G=\lambda^2,\; F=0,
$$

即参数化在局部为**共形映射**（保角）。

![等温坐标](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116175353721.png)

> **图注（中文教材）**：曲面参数化在局部为共形映射（保角）的参数称为等温参数（isothermal coordinates），等温参数化有如下形式 [正方形曲率线网图示]。我们知道，上述正方形是曲率线把曲面分成无穷小的正方形网格。

**Weierstrass–Enneper 表示**：设 $f$ 为全纯函数，$g$ 为亚纯函数，$z=u+iv$，则

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf{X}(u,v) = \operatorname{Re}\int \bigl(1-g^2,\; i(1+g^2),\; 2g\bigr)\, f\,\mathrm{d}z
}
$$

给出极小曲面参数化。收敛性证明中常用的等价写法（$g'(z)\neq 0$）为

$$
\mathbf{F} = \operatorname{Re}\int \frac{1}{g'(z)}
\begin{pmatrix}
1-g(z)^2 \\
i\bigl(1+g(z)^2\bigr) \\
2g(z)
\end{pmatrix}
\mathrm{d}z.
$$

> **图注（中文教材）**：我们记 $z = u+iv$，简记 $\partial = \partial/\partial z$。W-E 表示定理：设 $f, g$ 是定义在复平面上某区域上的全纯函数（holomorphic functions）和亚纯函数（meromorphic functions），那么由
>
> $$\mathbf{F} = \operatorname{Re}\int \bigl(1-g^2,\; i(1+g^2),\; 2g\bigr)\, f\,\mathrm{d}z$$
>
> 给出的三维欧氏空间中的曲面为极小曲面。

### 1.4 共形结构与 Christoffel 对偶

**等温浸入** $f:\mathbb{R}^2\supset D\to\mathbb{R}^3$ 的特征方程为

$$
\|f_x\|=\|f_y\|,\quad f_x\perp f_y,\quad f_{xy}\in\operatorname{span}\{f_x,f_y\}.
$$

几何意义：曲率线把曲面分成无穷小正方形。**Christoffel 变换**（对偶等温曲面）为

$$
f^*_x = \frac{f_x}{\|f_x\|^2}, \qquad f^*_y = -\frac{f_y}{\|f_y\|^2}.
$$

**Christoffel 定理**：极小曲面是等温的；等温浸入为极小曲面，当且仅当其**对偶浸入落在球面内**——此时对偶浸入恰为**Gauss 映射**（法线映射）。

![共形结构与极小曲面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116175622889.png)

> **图注（中文教材）**：曲面在局部参数为等温参数时被称为等温曲面（isothermic surface）[球面正交圆图案图示]。高斯像把局部曲率线网映到球面正交坐标系。

![Christoffel变换与对偶](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116175831462.png)

> **图注（中文教材）**：可以定义对偶的等温曲面（Christoffel 变换）：如果 $f:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}^3$ 是等温浸入，那么其对偶浸入（dual）定义为
>
> $$\partial_x f^{*} = \frac{\partial_x f}{\|\partial_x f\|^{2}}, \qquad \partial_y f^{*} = -\frac{\partial_y f}{\|\partial_y f\|^{2}}.$$
>
> 极小曲面特征——Christoffel 定理：等温浸入 $f$ 为极小曲面，当且仅当其对偶浸入 $f^{*}$ 落在球面内。

## 2. 离散框架：从组合到几何

### 2.1 总体逻辑

![总体逻辑流程](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116180605851.png)

> **图注**：**Figure 11.** The geometric idea of the construction of discrete minimal surfaces.

构造离散极小曲面的四步（与论文 Fig. 11 一致）：

1. **Gauss 像**：连续极小曲面的曲率线经 Gauss 映射映到 $S^2$，得球面四边形剖分；
2. **正交圆图案**：由组合数据求球面（或平面）正交圆图案（Koebe 定理保证存在性）；
3. **Koebe 多面体**：由圆图案构造棱切单位球的凸多面体；
4. **对偶化**：S-等温对偶 → 离散极小曲面。

### 2.2 离散等温曲面

**定义（离散等温）**：多面体曲面每个面为**共形正方形（conformal square）**——圆内接四边形，边视为复数 $a,a',b,b'$ 时

$$
\frac{aa'}{bb'} = -1.
$$

**Christoffel 对偶（离散）**：若边可一致标为 $+$ / $-$，则

$$
\Delta f^* = \pm \frac{\Delta f}{\|\Delta f\|^2}.
$$

复数形式（Lemma 1）：若 $a+b+a'+b'=0$ 且 $aa'/(bb')=-1$，则 $a^*=1/\bar{a}$ 等给出对偶共形正方形。

![等温曲面定义](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116180922644.png)

> **图注**：**DEFINITION 1.** A polyhedral surface is called *discrete conformal*, or *discrete isothermic*, if for every face the four edges can be viewed as complex numbers $a, a', b, b'$ with
>
> $$a + a' + b + b' = 0, \qquad \frac{aa'}{bb'} = -1.$$
>
> Such a face is called a *conformal square*.

![离散等温曲面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181204177.png)

> **图注**：**Figure 4.** A discrete isothermic surface, dual faces, and a coarser discretization of a smooth isothermic surface.

![Christoffel变换定义](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181236475.png)

> **图注**：**DEFINITION 2.** Two discrete isothermic surfaces are called *Christoffel duals*, if for each edge $\Delta f$ of one surface the edge $\Delta f^{*}$ of the dual surface satisfies
>
> $$\Delta f^{*} = \pm \frac{\Delta f}{\|\Delta f\|^{2}}$$
>
> with a consistent choice of signs.

### 2.3 S-quad-graph 与离散 S-等温曲面

**S-quad-graph**：偶四边形剖分 $D$，顶点染黑/白；白顶点再分两类——$s$（球）与 $c$（圆），每个四边形含各类白顶点各一。

**定义（离散 S-等温）**：映射

$$
f: D \to \{\text{空间中的圆、球及其交点}\}
$$

使得：$f(s)$ 为球、$f(c)$ 为圆、黑点为交点；同一面内圆与球**正交相交**于接触点。

**中心扩张（central extension）**：取球心、圆心及正交交点构成四边形，为**直角风筝形**，亦为共形正方形 → 得到离散等温曲面。

![S-quad-graph](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181312578.png)

> **图注**：**Figure 5.** An S-quad-graph with two cell decompositions of the sphere (left) and a generic face (right). The black vertex is the intersection point of a circle and a sphere that are orthogonal to each other and meet there.

![离散S-等温曲面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181402429.png)

> **图注**：**Figure 7.** A discrete S-isothermic surface. Note that the points on circles and the centers of spheres form a discrete isothermic surface. The right hand side shows the dual S-isothermic surface.

**几何 → 组合**：删去与 $c$ 关联的边，得胞腔剖分 $G$：顶点对应球，面内接圆且相邻圆相切——**多面体离散 S-等温曲面**。

![几何结构到组合结构](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181345119.png)

> **图注**：**Figure 6.** The passage from geometry to combinatorics: deleting the c-circles. The cell decomposition of the sphere has a circle inscribed in each face.

### 2.4 四圆共球面（touching coins lemma）

**引理**：空间中四圆两两循环相切且**不共球**时，过四个切点的球面与四圆**正交相交**。故 $n=4$ 时可省略显式正交条件。

![四圆共球面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181655217.png)

> **图注**：**LEMMA 2 (Four touching coins).** Four mutually touching circles in space which do not lie in one sphere define a sphere S which passes through the four touching points and intersects all four circles orthogonally.

**推论（Christoffel 对偶的半径关系）**：设平面 $2n$ 边形边向量为 $\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_{2n}$，$\sum_j\ell_j=0$，内切圆半径 $R$，顶点 $j$ 到切点距离 $r_j$ 满足 $\|\ell_j\|=r_j+r_{j+1}$。则对偶边

$$
\ell^*_j = (-1)^j \frac{1}{r_j\,r_{j+1}}\,\ell_j
$$

构成内切圆半径 $1/R$ 的多边形。**球与圆的半径在对偶中互为倒数**。

![推论](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181832872.png)

> **图注**：**COROLLARY 3.** Let the sides of a planar $2n$-gon be vectors $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_{2n}$ with $\sum_j \ell_j = 0$, inscribed in a circle of radius $R$, and let $r_j$ be the distance from vertex $j$ to the touching point with the circle. Then
>
> $$\ell^{*}_j = (-1)^j \frac{1}{r_j\,r_{j+1}}\,\ell_j$$
>
> defines the sides of a $2n$-gon inscribed in a circle of radius $1/R$. The formula for a 3D polygon is similar, except that the signs alternate as in the 2D case.

**圆填充与三角化**：球面圆填充 ↔ 三角剖分（圆↔顶点，相切↔边）；三角情形还自动产生**第二族正交圆**（过三圆切点的圆与三圆正交）。

![圆填充与三角化](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116181918566.png)

> **图注**：**Figure 8.** A circle packing on the sphere corresponds to a triangulation. The triangulation has a vertex for each circle, and an edge for each pair of touching circles. For a triangulation there is automatically also a second family of circles orthogonal to the first one (Figure 9).

## 3. Koebe 多面体

### 3.1 Koebe 定理（定理 1）

**定理 1（Koebe, 1936）**：对球面每个三角剖分，存在球面圆填充：圆↔顶点，两圆相切当且仅当顶点相邻；**在 Möbius 变换下唯一**。

![Koebe定理基本形式](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182007579.png)

> **图注**：**THEOREM 1 (Koebe 1936).** For every triangulation of the sphere there is a circle packing on the sphere with the circles corresponding to the vertices, and two circles touching if and only if the corresponding vertices are adjacent. The circle packing is unique up to Möbius transformations.

### 3.2 正交圆图案（定理 2）

**定理 2**：对球面每个**多面体型（polytopal）**胞腔剖分，存在圆图案：

- 每个**顶点**、每个**面**各对应一圆；
- 顶点圆构成圆填充（相邻顶点 ↔ 相切圆）；
- 面圆亦构成圆填充（相邻面 ↔ 相切圆）；
- 每条边处：一对顶点圆与一对面圆在同点相切，且**彼此正交**。

图案在 Möbius 变换下唯一。变分构造见 [Bobenko–Springborn 圆图案能量](2016-10-01-圆形填充视角.md)。

![Koebe多边形与S-等温曲面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182048755.png)

> **图注**：The polytopal cell decomposition of the sphere is the combinatorics of an S-isothermic surface.

### 3.3 Koebe 多面体（定理 3）

**定理 3**：每个球面多面体型剖分可实现为**棱切单位球**的凸多面体；在**固定球面的射影变换**下唯一。对偶多面体可同时实现，对应棱在同点切球且**正交**。

构造：将 $s$ 圆提升为与 $S^2$ 正交相交的球，连球心得棱切多面体；$c$ 圆内接于各面 → **Koebe 多面体** = 一种离散 S-等温曲面。

![定理2](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182116501.png)

> **图注**：**THEOREM 2.** For every polytopal¹ cellular decomposition of the sphere, there exists a pattern of circles in the sphere with the following properties. There is a circle corresponding to each face and to each vertex. The vertex circles form a packing with two circles touching if and only if the corresponding vertices are adjacent. Likewise, the face circles form a packing with circles touching if and only if the corresponding faces are adjacent. For each edge, there is a pair of touching vertex circles and a pair of touching face circles. These pairs touch in the same point, intersecting each other orthogonally. This circle pattern is unique up to Möbius transformations.

![定理3](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182255818.png)

> **图注**：**THEOREM 3.** Every polytopal cell decomposition of the sphere can be realized by a polyhedron with edges tangent to the sphere. This realization is unique up to projective transformations which fix the sphere. ¹We call a cellular decomposition of a surface polytopal, if the closed cells are closed discs, and two closed cells intersect in one closed cell if at all. There is a simultaneous realization of the dual polyhedron, such that corresponding edges of the dual and the original polyhedron touch the sphere in the same points and intersect orthogonally.

## 4. 离散极小曲面

### 4.1 Christoffel 对偶定理（定理 4–5）

**定理 4（Christoffel）**：极小曲面 ⊂ 等温曲面；等温浸入为极小曲面 ⟺ 对偶浸入 ⊂ 球面（= Gauss 映射）。

**定义（离散极小曲面）**：离散 S-等温曲面 $F:Q\to\mathbb{R}^3$ 满足：对球心白顶点 $x$，邻点 $y_j$ 为切点/交点，$F(y_j)=F(x)+b_j$，则下列等价：

1. 点 $F(x)+(-1)^j b_j$ 共圆；
2. 存在 $N\in\mathbb{R}^3$ 使 $(-1)^j\langle b_j,N\rangle$ 对所有 $j$ 相同；
3. 过 $F(x)$ 的某平面两侧，偶/奇指标切点分别落在与其等距的平行平面上。

![Christoffel对偶定理](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182350323.png)

> **图注**：**4. Discrete minimal surfaces.** The following theorem about continuous minimal surfaces is due to Christoffel [8]. For a modern treatment, see [15]. This theorem is the basis for our definition of discrete minimal surfaces. We provide a short proof for the reader's convenience. **THEOREM 4 (Christoffel).** Minimal surfaces are isothermic. An isothermic immersion is a minimal surface, if and only if the dual immersion is contained in a sphere. In that case the dual immersion is in fact the Gauss map of the minimal surface, up to scale and translation.

**定理 5**：离散 S-等温曲面为离散极小曲面 ⟺ 其对偶离散 S-等温曲面为 **Koebe 多面体**（所有球正交相交于同一固定球，所有圆落在此球上）。

![S-等温曲面与离散极小曲面](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182613819.png)

> **图注**：**THEOREM 5.** An S-isothermic discrete surface is a discrete minimal surface, if and only if the dual S-isothermic surface corresponds to a Koebe polyhedron.

![离散极小曲面构造](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182702084.png)

> **图注（定理 5 证明）**：Proof. That the $S$-isothermic dual of a Koebe polyhedron is a discrete minimal surface is fairly obvious. On the other hand, let $F: Q \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be a discrete minimal surface and let $x \in Q$ and $y_1 \dots y_{2n} \in Q$ be as in Definition 6. Let $\widetilde F: Q \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be the dual $S$-isothermic surface. We need to show that all circles of $\widetilde F$ lie in one and the same sphere $S$ and that all the spheres of $\widetilde F$ intersect $S$ orthogonally. It follows immediately from Definition 6 that the points $\widetilde F(y_1)\dots\widetilde F(y_{2n})$ lie on a circle $c_x$ in a sphere $S_x$ around $\widetilde F(x)$. Let $S$ be the sphere which intersects $S_x$ orthogonally in $c_x$. The orthogonal circles through $\widetilde F(y_1)\dots\widetilde F(y_{2n})$ also lie in $S$. Hence, all spheres of $\widetilde F$ intersect $S$ orthogonally and all circles of $\widetilde F$ lie in $S$.

## 5. 离散 Weierstrass 表示

设 $Q$ 为 S-quad-graph，$c:Q\to\mathbb{C}$ 为**平面正交圆图案**（白点 $x$：圆心 $c(x)$；黑点 $y$：交点 $c(y)$）。立体投影

$$
s(p)=\frac{1}{1+|p|^2}
\begin{pmatrix} 2\operatorname{Re}p \\ 2\operatorname{Im}p \\ |p|^2-1 \end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\mathrm{d}s_p(v)=\operatorname{Re}\!\left(\frac{2\bar{v}}{(1+|p|^2)^2}
\begin{pmatrix} 1-p^2 \\ i(1+p^2) \\ 2p \end{pmatrix}\right).
$$

对相邻球心 $x_1,x_2$（标签 $s$），中间黑点 $y$，$p=c(y)$，球半径

$$
R(x_j)=\left|\frac{1+|c(x_j)|^2-|c(x_j)-p|^2}{2|c(x_j)-p|}\right|.
$$

**定理 8（离散 Weierstrass）**：对应离散极小曲面的球心满足

$$
\boxed{
F(x_2)-F(x_1)=\pm\operatorname{Re}\!\left(
\frac{R(x_2)+R(x_1)}{1+|p|^2}\,
\frac{c(x_2)-c(x_1)}{|c(x_2)-c(x_1)|}\,
\begin{pmatrix} 1-p^2 \\ i(1+p^2) \\ 2p \end{pmatrix}
\right)
}
\tag{4}
$$

符号由连接 $x_1,y,x_2$ 的两条边标记 $+/-$ 决定。

![离散Weierstrass表示](https://lgximgs.oss-cn-beijing.aliyuncs.com/images/image-20260116182717713.png)

> **图注**：**THEOREM 6 (Weierstrass representation).** Let $Q$ be an $S$-quad-graph, and let $c: Q \to \mathbb{C}$ be an orthogonal circle pattern in the plane. For white vertices $x \in Q$, $c(x)$ is the center of the corresponding circle, and for black vertices $y \in Q$, $c(y)$ is the corresponding intersection point. The $S$-isothermic minimal surface
>
> $$F: \{x \in Q \mid x \text{ is labelled } \mathbb{S}\} \to \mathbb{R}^3, \qquad F(x) = \text{the center of the sphere corresponding to } x$$
>
> that corresponds to this circle pattern is given by the following formula. Let $x_1, x_2 \in Q$ be two vertices, both labelled $\mathbb{S}$, that correspond to touching circles of the pattern, and let $y \in Q$ be the black vertex between $x_1$ and $x_2$, which corresponds to the point of contact. The centers $F(x_1)$ and $F(x_2)$ of the corresponding touching spheres of the $S$-isothermic minimal surface $F$ satisfy
>
> $$\tag{4} F(x_2) - F(x_1) = \pm \operatorname{Re}\!\left( \frac{R(x_2) + R(x_1)}{1 + |p|^2}\cdot \frac{\overline{c(x_2) - c(x_1)}}{|c(x_2) - c(x_1)|}\begin{pmatrix} 1 - p^2 \\ i(1 + p^2) \\ 2p \end{pmatrix} \right),$$
>
> where $p = c(y)$ and the radii $R(x_j)$ of the spheres are
>
> $$\tag{5} R(x_j) = \left| \frac{1 + |c(x_j)|^2 - |c(x_j) - p|^2}{2\,|c(x_j) - p|} \right|.$$
>
> The sign on the right-hand side of equation (4) depends on whether the two edges of the quad-graph connecting $x_1$ with $y$ and $y$ with $x_2$ are labelled '$+$' or '$-$' (see Figures 4 and 5 (right)).

**关联族**：对偶化前将 Koebe 多面体每条棱在平面内旋转角 $\varphi$，得一族离散曲面 $F_\varphi$；边长公式中乘 $e^{i\varphi}$。悬链面与正螺面为连续关联族的同一例；离散悬链面的关联族见图 17。

## 6. 收敛性

**定理 11**：设 $F:W\to\mathbb{R}^3$ 为无脐点的极小浸入，$(u,v)$ 为共形曲率线坐标，$D\Subset W$ 单连通。则存在 S-等温离散极小曲面序列 $F_n:Q_n\to\mathbb{R}$，$Q_n\subset\frac{1}{n}\mathbb{Z}^2\cap D$，使

$$
\|F_n-F\|_{L^\infty(K)} = O\!\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)
$$

对 $D$ 中紧集 $K$ 成立；**整个关联族**亦一致收敛，误差同为 $O(1/n)$。

证明要点：Schramm 正交圆图案收敛于全纯数据 $(g,g')$；再代入公式 (4)。平面圆图案即离散全纯数据，对应连续 Weierstrass 中的 $g$。

> **图注**：**7. Convergence.** Schramm has proved the convergence of circle patterns with the combinatorics of the square grid to meromorphic functions [26]. Together with the Weierstrass-type representation formula for $S$-isothermic minimal surfaces, this implies the following approximation theorem for discrete minimal surfaces. Figure 13 illustrates the convergence of $S$-isothermic Enneper surfaces to the continuous Enneper surface. **THEOREM 9.** Let $D \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a simply connected bounded domain with smooth boundary, and let $W \subset \mathbb{C}$ be an open set that contains the closure of $D$. Suppose that $F: W \to \mathbb{R}^3$ is a minimal immersion without umbilic points in conformal curvature line coordinates. There exists a sequence of $S$-isothermic minimal surfaces $F_n: Q_n \to \mathbb{R}^3$ such that the following holds. Each $Q_n$ is a simply connected $S$-quad-graph in $D$ which is a subset of the lattice $\frac{1}{n}\mathbb{Z}^2$. If, for $x \in D$, $\widehat F_n(x)$ is the value of $F_n$ at a point of $Q_n$ closest to $x$, then $\widehat F_n$ converges to $F$ uniformly with error $O(\frac{1}{n})$ on compacts in $D$. In fact, the whole associated families $\widehat F_{n,\varphi}$ converge to the associated family $F_\varphi$ of $F$ uniformly (also in $\varphi$) and with error $O(\frac{1}{n})$ on compacts in $D$. *Proof.* When $F$ is appropriately scaled,
>
> $$\tag{7} F = \operatorname{Re}\int \begin{pmatrix} 1 - g(z)^2 \\ i(1 + g(z)^2) \\ 2g(z) \end{pmatrix} \frac{dz}{g'(z)},$$
>
> where $g: W \to \mathbb{C}$ is a locally injective meromorphic function. By Schramm's results (Theorem 9.1 of [26] and the remark on p. 387), there exists a sequence of orthogonal circle patterns $c_n: Q_n \to \mathbb{C}$ approximating $g$ and $g'$ uniformly and with error $O(\frac{1}{n})$ on compacts in $D$. Define $F_n$ by the Weierstrass formula (4) with data $c = c_n$. Using the notation of Theorem 6, one finds
>
> $$\frac{1}{n^2}\bigl(F_n(x_2) - F_n(x_1)\bigr) \xrightarrow{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{n} \begin{pmatrix} 1 - g(y)^2 \\ i(1 + g(y)^2) \\ 2g(y) \end{pmatrix} \cdot \frac{1}{g'(y)} + O\!\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right)$$
>
> uniformly on compacts in $D$. After rescaling $\widehat F_n = \frac{1}{n} F_n$, the convergence claim follows. The same reasoning applies to the whole associated family of $F$.

## 7. 经典例子的构造

下表给出连续情形的 Weierstrass 数据 $(f,g)$（§1.3 记号），离散情形则用**平面正交圆图案**替代全纯数据，再经立体投影 + Koebe 多面体 + 对偶化。

| 曲面 | Weierstrass 数据 $(f,\,g)$ | 离散圆图案来源 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enneper** | $f=1,\; g=z$ | 等半径圆，圆心位于方格点 |
| **Catenoid / Helicoid** | $f=1,\; g=1/z$（关联族参数 $\varphi$ 连接二者） | S-Exp 图案（离散指数映射） |
| **$m$-Enneper** | $f=1,\; g=z^a$ | 离散 $z^a$ 圆图案 [Bobenko–Pinkall '99] |
| **Schwarz-P** | Gauss 映射为二重覆盖，8 个分支点 | 有限圆图案（细化立方体曲率线网） |
| **Scherk tower** | 单周期，渐近两相交平面 | 有限圆图案，端点处 $z\mapsto z^2$ 型组合 |

### 7.1 Enneper 曲面

**连续参数化**（取 $f=1,\,g=z$，$z=u+iv$）：

$$
\mathbf{X}(u,v)=\left(
u-\frac{u^3}{3}+uv^2,\;
v-\frac{v^3}{3}+vu^2,\;
u^2-v^2
\right).
$$

Gauss 映射将曲率线映为球面上的**经纬网**；离散版取球面上（或平面上的）最简单正交圆图案。

**离散构造**（论文 §7.1）：平面上取半径均为 $r$ 的圆，圆心落在方格点上，间距为 $\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\,r$。该图案正交（Schramm 方格圆图案的标准退化情形）。立体投影到 $S^2$，对其中一半圆构造正交球面，再 **Christoffel 对偶** → 离散 Enneper 曲面（论文 Fig. 1 左、Fig. 18 收敛序列）。

### 7.2 Catenoid 与关联族

**连续 Catenoid**（$f=1,\,g=1/z$）：

$$
\mathbf{X}(u,v)=\left(\cosh v\cos u,\;\cosh v\sin u,\;v\right).
$$

**关联族**中参数 $\varphi\in[0,\pi/2]$ 连接 Catenoid（$\varphi=0$）与 Helicoid（$\varphi=\pi/2$），等距变形且 Gauss 映射不变。

**离散 S-Exp 圆图案**（论文 §7.2）：底图 $Q=\mathbb{Z}^2$，圆对应格点 $(m,n)$ 且 $m+n\equiv 0\pmod 2$。圆心与半径显式为

$$
c(n,m)=e^{\alpha n + i\rho m}, \qquad
r(n,m)=\sin(\rho)\,|c(n,m)|,
$$

其中

$$
\rho=\frac{\pi}{N}, \qquad
\alpha=\operatorname{arctanh}\!\left(\frac{1}{2}\bigl|1-e^{2i\rho}\bigr|\right).
$$

这是复指数映射 $z\mapsto e^z$ 的离散正交圆图案（Bobenko–Pinkall S-Exp）。立体投影 + 对偶 → **离散悬链面**（Fig. 1 右）；将关联族旋转角 $\varphi$ 代入公式 (4) 的 $e^{i\varphi}$ 因子，得悬链面到正螺面的离散形变（Fig. 17）。

> 另有 [Bobenko–Pinkall '96] 的离散等温悬链面、Polthier–Rossman 的面积极小三角悬链面，与 S-等温版本**不等价**——后者保持共形/圆图案结构。

### 7.3 高阶 Enneper 曲面（$m$-Enneper）

**连续**：Weierstrass 数据中将 $g=z$ 换为 $g=z^a$（$a\in\mathbb{R}^+$），中心出现**脐点**（umbilic）。

**离散**：采用 [Bobenko–Pinkall '99] 中 $z\mapsto z^a$ 的正交圆图案扇区；[Agafonov–Bobenko] 证明了嵌入性。立体投影 + 对偶 → S-等温高阶 Enneper。论文展示 $a=4/3$（简单脐点）的情形（Fig. 13）。

### 7.4 Schwarz-P 曲面

**连续**：三重周期极小曲面；基本域为亏格 3 的 Riemann 面；**Gauss 映射**为球面的**二重覆盖**，8 个分支点。曲率线在球面上的像为**细化的立方体网格**（Fig. 11 左上）。

**离散构造**（论文 §7.4）：

1. 三个方向各取 $m,n,k$ 个切片（均为**偶数**），立方体 8 个角点为分支点 → 每角点 6 条边（标 $c$）；
2. 暂时忽略二重覆盖，用定理 2 求球面正交圆图案（Fig. 11 右上，示八分之一）；
3. 施加**八面体度量对称**消除 Möbius 歧义；
4. 提升到二重覆盖，构造 Koebe 多面体，对偶化 → Schwarz-P 曲面（Fig. 11 底行、Fig. 2 左）。

**定理 6**：给定三个偶正整数 $m,n,k$，存在对应的（一般非对称）S-等温 Schwarz-P 曲面，且唯一。$m:n:k$ 相同比例对应同一连续 Schwarz-P 的不同离散化；$m=n=k$ 为对称 Schwarz-P。

### 7.5 Scherk tower

**连续**：单周期极小曲面，渐近两相交平面；Gauss 像中曲率线网有 4 个特殊点（**半平面端**）。绕特殊点一圈 = 曲面一个周期；邻域内曲率线像为 $z\mapsto z^2$ 下的标准坐标网。

**离散组合**（论文 §7.5、Fig. 14）：

- 特殊点用**两对 3-价顶点**建模；
- 将每对 3-价顶点**分裂**为两个，中间插入 2-价顶点；新边长度取 0 → 对偶后出现**无穷长棱**（对应半平面端）；
- 组合结构与某组 Schwarz-P 参数（一方向 $k=2$）相同，但特殊顶点**不作分支点**解释；
- 由 Koebe 定理 + 八面体对称得圆图案，对偶化 → Scherk tower（Fig. 2 右）。

**定理 7**：给定两个偶正整数 $m,n$，存在对应 S-等温 Scherk tower，且唯一。$m=n$ 时渐近平面正交（最对称 Scherk tower）。

> **图注**：**PROPOSITION 4.** The radii $r_j$ are the correct radii for the circle pattern if and only if the corresponding $\rho_j$ satisfy the following equations, one for each circle: The equation for circle $j$ is
>
> $$\tag{9} 2\sum_{\text{neighbors }k}\bigl(\arctan e^{\rho_k - \rho_j} + \arctan e^{\rho_k + \rho_j}\bigr) = \Phi_j,$$
>
> where the sum is taken over all neighboring circles $k$. For each circle $j$, $\Phi_j$ is the nominal angle covered by the neighboring circles. It is normally $2\pi$ for interior circles, but it differs for circles on the boundary or for circles where the pattern branches. The equations (9) are the equations for a critical point of the functional
>
> $$S(\rho) = \sum_{(j,k)}\bigl(\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Li}_2(ie^{\rho_k - \rho_j}) + \operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Li}_2(ie^{\rho_j - \rho_k}) - \operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Li}_2(ie^{\rho_j + \rho_k}) - \operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Li}_2(ie^{-\rho_j - \rho_k}) - \pi(\rho_j + \rho_k)\bigr) + \sum_j \Phi_j \rho_j,$$
>
> where the first sum is over all pairs $(j,k)$ of neighboring circles and the second sum over all circles $j$. The dilogarithm $\operatorname{Li}_2(z)$ is defined by $\operatorname{Li}_2(z) = -\int_0^z \log(1-\zeta)\,d\zeta/\zeta$. The second derivative of $S(\rho)$ is the quadratic form
>
> $$\tag{10} D^2S = \sum_{(j,k)}\!\left(\frac{1}{\cosh(\rho_k - \rho_j)}(d\rho_k - d\rho_j)^2 - \frac{1}{\cosh(\rho_k + \rho_j)}(d\rho_k + d\rho_j)^2\right),$$
>
> where the sum is taken over pairs of neighboring circles.
